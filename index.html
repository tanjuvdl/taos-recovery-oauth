TAOS Encrypted Recovery

TAOS Encrypted Recovery is a personal, single-user backup utility. It creates and verifies a client-side encrypted recovery repository in Google Drive.

Privacy

The tool accesses Google Drive only to create, read, update, and remove encrypted recovery objects in its dedicated repository. It does not upload unencrypted TAOS material, sell data, use advertising, or share data with third parties. The encryption password remains in the user’s local macOS Keychain and is never transmitted to this site or stored in Google Drive.

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer through the Google OAuth consent screen contact address.

Terms

This is a personal recovery utility. It is used only by its account owner for encrypted backup and restoration. The owner remains responsible for retaining independent backups and for safeguarding local device and account access.
